## Continuity and callback locks

- Leo recruits Yoshimi to tell Otome he left for abacus lessons. Kinu proposes
  the lie; Otome believes it and regrets another missed conversation. Erika warns
  Leo will suffer if it is exposed.
- Erika's umbrella exchange ends with her declaring she already decided to walk
  home with Yoppi. Preserve the light possessive affection without adding romance.
- In the rain, Leo shares with Kinu and Subaru shares with Fukahire. Subaru lets
  his own right side get soaked to keep Fukahire completely dry; this shows why
  Leo cannot understand people finding Subaru frightening.
- Kinu's attempts to avoid rain (crowding, piggyback) are self-serving. She is
  gathering information to attack Otome's psychological weaknesses after losing
  by fair means; reports so far only establish Otome's prowess, academics, and
  respected/feared `big sis` reputation.
- Subaru charms the produce vendor for a free pear, then meets Yoshimi shopping.
  Both live alone and cook for themselves. His offer to dispatch Leo or Crab if
  she gets lonely is friendly.
- At dinner Subaru cooks bonito and asparagus kinpira. His `young wife targeted
  by a lecherous rice dealer` description is a stock melodramatic image, not an
  actual threat. He evaluates Yoshimi as supportive but perhaps too mild.
- Subaru's list `Yoppi, Princess, or Director Tachibana` ends in a deliberate
  absurd option that Leo fails to retort to; Subaru calls out the missed punchline.
