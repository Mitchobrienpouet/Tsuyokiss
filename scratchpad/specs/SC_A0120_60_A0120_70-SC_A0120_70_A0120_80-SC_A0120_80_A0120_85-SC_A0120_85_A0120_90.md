## Reveal and continuity locks

  remedial students. Keep Inori's insult `馴れ合いお友達ペア` and the friends'
  denial/teasing rhythm.
- `SC_A0120_70_A0120_80` is a repeated temptation/concentration gag: Inori
  deliberately distracts Leo, then Tsuchinaga pecks him. Keep the escalating
  sequence (breasts -> pencil lead -> fugashi) and do not sanitize the ordinary
  suggestive comedy into explicit description.
- `SC_A0120_80_A0120_85` resolves the lesson sincerely: Inori ultimately teaches
  well and notices Leo falling behind in English.
- `SC_A0120_85_A0120_90` establishes Matsukasa as the curry city and Oasis as
  Kinu's workplace. Preserve `Princess` only when `姫` appears elsewhere; it does
  not occur in this shard.
- The Curry King is introduced first as `Woman`, then nicknamed `King`. Do not
  identify her beyond the source. Shinichi's tea gambit fails twice: Kinu serves
  it scalding hot, and King likes it.
- The manager's India/America/New Delhi reversals are deliberate nonsense. Keep
  the final uncertainty and Leo's conclusion that the curry is good regardless.
