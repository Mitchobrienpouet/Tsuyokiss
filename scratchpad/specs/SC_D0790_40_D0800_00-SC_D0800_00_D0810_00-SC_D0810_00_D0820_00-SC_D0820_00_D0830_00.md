  at third. Erika returns refreshed, strikes out the side, and Class 2-C wins
  2-1. Preserve all baseball causality, the 5-minute rest, and Leo's injured
  palms.
- `SC_D0800_00_D0810_00` opens on the prize presentation and treatment. The
  team unanimously yields the mysterious trophy to Erika. Otome notices Leo's
  damaged hands and explicitly frames her concern as that of an older sister;
  Subaru explains to Kinu that Leo played through pain for Erika.

  of her hand as his reward, and later visits him with Yoshimi after he catches
  her cold. The hand kiss is courtly and mutually permitted; do not intensify

  refuses money as a substitute for personal value and lectures her about

  a principle she already holds, then privately denies his influence while
  immediately considering building a bottle ship herself. Keep the irony.

  half a year earlier: exchange dolls, keep their souls together, never lie to
  each other, help each other, and remain lifelong friends. This is an earnest
  friendship oath and continuity anchor for Yoshimi's later insight into Erika.

  She explicitly likes him as himself and does not want sudden grind-school
