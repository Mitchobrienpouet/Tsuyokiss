## Four-scene continuity

1. `SC_B0330_00_B0340_00` completes the computer lesson begun in the prior
   scene. Otome learns startup/shutdown by treating every mouse action like
   martial training. Leo's physical guidance makes him briefly conscious of
   her as a woman, but the scene returns to the older-sister bond: her rare
   smile, gratitude, head-patting, and promise to watch over him more closely.
   At the council computer she proudly repeats the procedure while Erika mocks
   her in baby talk; Otome is too guileless to notice.
2. `SC_B0340_00_B0350_00` pays off that promise by taking it too far. Otome
   commandeers Leo's entire weekend for chores, study, and training. Leo rebels,
   lies, and runs away; Otome first scoffs, then spends hours searching in real
   fear. At midnight they reconcile. Her earlier mistake with her younger
   brother Takuma explains the pattern, and Leo recognizes both her clumsy care
   and his own childishness. A council-room coda explicitly denies romance at
   this point and sends Otome to the library-building disturbance.
3. `SC_B0350_00_B0360_00` begins while Otome runs toward that disturbance.
   Leo meets shy publicity-committee photographer Noriko Nishizaki after Yohei
   Murata rebuffs her attempts to reconcile. Noriko climbs a tree to rescue a
   stranded cat; Leo helps, they fall, and the cat accidentally causes a kiss.
   Leo treats her scratched arm and learns that Yohei is not openly angry so
   much as embarrassed by public teasing. Leo urges her to approach Yohei
   privately, then reflects on his own childhood `Hustle Kid` experience: acting
   from righteous momentum got both him and the girl teased, which taught him
   self-protective conformity. He does not want Noriko to become similarly
   cynical. Erika and Otome arrive only after Noriko leaves.
4. `SC_B0360_00_B0370_00` immediately tests that resolve. Noriko offers Yohei
   homemade cookies, but another boy appears and Yohei humiliates her, scatters
   them, and leaves. Leo chooses intervention over self-protective conformity.
   Yohei's jealousy and inferiority surface as he beats Leo; Subaru intervenes
   out of fierce loyalty; Otome stops both fighters. Inori channels the unresolved
   conflict into the coming Sports Martial Arts Festival boxing tournament.
   Leo asks Otome to train him; she tests that the resolve is his own, then
   enthusiastically prescribes a far harsher regimen. The next scene begins the
   island training camp and establishes the plan: survive Yohei's `Gatling Gun`
   punches and land a left hook to the chin.

## Identity, reveal, and foreshadowing locks

- Do not reinterpret the Otome/Leo bond as mutual romance. `B0340:311-318`
  explicitly asks and denies that reading: she cares for him as a younger
  brother and rejects men who are merely kind but weak.
- `B0350:100` is an accidental kiss, caused by the cat landing on Noriko's head.
  Do not add desire, consent language, or relationship status beyond the source.
- Noriko and Yohei's conflict remains a misunderstanding driven by his shame,
  teasing, jealousy, and inferiority. `B0350:197-199` is Leo's inference, not an
  omniscient confirmation. `B0360:83-108` reveals jealousy but does not fully
  resolve the relationship.
- Leo's intervention at `B0360:46-66` deliberately reverses the lesson he drew
  from the `Hustle Kid` memory (`B0350:203-218`). Preserve the contradiction as
  character growth: he knows the social cost and acts anyway.
- The tournament setup is not a generic revenge arc. Leo's wounded pride, his
  defense of Noriko, and his refusal to let Subaru settle it for him all matter.
