This is a translation-stage handoff note, not QC or arbitration. All indexes
listed below are permitted projected rows.

  name (`素奈緒`) and `素直` (honest/straightforward) has no exact English
  equivalent. The translation makes the first turn explicit as `For someone
  named Sunao, you sure aren't very honest`, then uses `living up to your name`
  on the reprise. Accuracy/literary QC may prefer a less explanatory surface,
  but the two joke chains and repeated `off-limits` answer must remain linked.

  is rendered `Shin-san`. `Your pal Shin` is a defensible more fully localized
  alternative, but any revision must retain the odd third-person self-label
  rather than reducing it to ordinary `I`.
