# Targeted arbitration: SC_C0140_00_K0900_00

## Index 42 -- parody-name romanization

- Source: `あだし充`
- Current candidate: `Adashi Mitsuru`
- Alternative considered: normalize it to the real-world name being parodied.
- Independent reading: the kana deliberately says `あだし`, not `あだち`, so
  preserving `Adashi Mitsuru` carries the altered name without inventing or
  resolving the joke.
- Decision: retain `Adashi Mitsuru` on accuracy and source-form grounds.
