## Index 160

Japanese: `「そうよ、いるか。私が顧問弁護士をやることになった霧夜カンパニーのご令嬢」`

- Independent candidate, produced before comparison: `「That's right, Dolphin. She's the daughter of Kiriya Company, where I've been appointed legal counsel.」`
- Pre-arbitration translation: `「That's right, Iruka. She's the daughter of Kiriya Company, where I've just become legal counsel.」`
- Logged alternative: `「That's right. She's the daughter of Kiriya Company, where I've been appointed legal counsel.」`

Decision: **independent candidate**.

Reason: accuracy and the shard lock win the ladder. The comma and sentence break
make `いるか` an explicit direct address; omitting it drops source text. The
continuity spec explicitly locks `イルカ` as `Dolphin`, so an independent
romanization is not permitted. `やることになった` establishes an appointment,
while `just become` adds a temporal claim not fixed by the Japanese. The
selected line preserves the vocative and legal-counsel relationship without
leaking any later fact.
