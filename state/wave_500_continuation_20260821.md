## Fail-closed source rule

A scene may be claimed for model work only when its authoritative Japanese source is locally materialized and the permitted projection can be generated by `tools/codex_vn_pipeline.py`. If the source or required Codex runtime is unavailable, do not infer, back-translate, reconstruct, or fabricate Japanese. Record the exact blocker and perform only safe recovery/audit work until the authoritative source is available.

## Rotation rule

Lanes rotate over consecutive open shards. As soon as a scene finishes translation, its two independent QC lenses begin; arbitration and deterministic gates follow before that scene is counted as closed. The supervisor always resumes from durable Git state, not from stale campaign counters.

## Completion condition

Campaign completes when either:

1. 500 additional admissible scenes have been fully closed after this campaign launch; or
2. every remaining admissible scene in the authoritative recovered corpus has been fully closed.

No already-closed scene may be counted toward this Wave-500 target.
