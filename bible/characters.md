# Character and voice locks

## Leo Tsushima (対馬レオ)

Second-year protagonist and first-person narrator. Casual, quick-witted,
self-deprecating, and comfortable trading affectionate insults with old friends.
His narration should read briskly, never like formal literary exposition.

## Kinu Kanisawa (蟹沢きぬ / きぬ / カニ)

Leo's childhood friend. Loud, shameless, impulsive, competitive, and highly
comic. Preserve her bold self-regard and rapid retorts. `Crab` is her locked
nickname when カニ is used as direct address.

## Subaru Date (伊達スバル / スバル)

Leo's male childhood friend. Laid-back, capable, teasing, and rough-edged without
being hostile. Use relaxed contractions and masculine banter; avoid caricatured
dialect.

## Otome Kurogane (鉄乙女)

Leo's older cousin and a third-year. Disciplined, formidable, morally direct,
and commanding. Her speech is clean, concise, and unflustered. Before the name
reveal, generic tags such as 上級生 remain `Upperclassman`.

## Erika Kiriya (霧夜エリカ)

Student council president. Elegant, imperious, playful, and socially dominant.
Her polished diction can turn sharply teasing without becoming generic slang.

## Nagomi Yashi (椰子なごみ)

Tall first-year who keeps others at a distance. Uses outwardly polite senior
address with conspicuous coldness, except toward Kinu; recurring blunt insults
must stay character-specific.

## Inori Oeyama (大江山祈)

Teacher and student-council adviser. Adult, composed, enigmatic, and knowingly
alluring; do not make her voice girlish or over-explain her implications.

## Yoshimi Sato (佐藤良美)

Second-year and Erika's close aide. Friendly and helpful on the surface, with
important tonal undercurrents that must not be exposed before the source does.

## Kinu's mother (マダム)

Warm, deadpan, and utterly willing to roast her own daughter. Her jokes land
through matter-of-fact delivery, not exaggerated cruelty.

## Announcer (アナウンサー)

Neutral broadcast register.
